Space Details & Amenities

Designed as a flexible blank canvas, The Great Hall adapts easily from morning civic pancake breakfasts and daytime board retreats to evening concerts, lectures, and seated dinners.

Main Hall & Stage

  • Dimensions: Approx. 60′ × 40′ (~2,400 sq. ft. main floor) Placeholder
  • Auditorium Capacity: Up to 350 guests in theater/row seating
  • Dining Capacity: Up to 200 guests at banquet tables
  • Stage: Elevated, curtained, and lit stage for speakers, panels, musicals, and recitals
  • Climate Control: Fully air-conditioned in summer and heated in winter

Commercial Kitchen

  • Full-Scale Prep Space: Spacious commercial-grade kitchen directly adjacent to the Great Hall
  • Cooking & Warming: Commercial ranges, ovens, and generous stainless steel prep counters
  • Cleanup & Refrigeration: Commercial sinks, dishwashing station, and refrigerator/freezer space
  • Ideal For: Rotary & civic pancake breakfasts, community fundraisers, and outside caterers
  • Catering Friendly: Bring your preferred licensed caterer with no exclusive vendor lock-in

Equipment & Event Types

  • Furnishings Included: Folding banquet tables and chairs included with standard hall rental
  • Connectivity & A/V: High-speed Wi-Fi, podium, microphone/sound system, and projection options
  • Nonprofits & Associations: Chamber mixers, guild exhibitions, arts foundation galas, and club meetings
  • Education & Workshops: Training days, lectures, rehearsals, and community classes
  • Private Gatherings: Milestone celebrations, luncheons, repasts, and rehearsal dinners

Availability, Parking & Venue Guidelines

Everything you need to know about scheduling, downtown Ridgewood parking, and facility policies.

Available Booking Windows

The Great Hall is available for single-day events and recurring group bookings during the following windows:

  • Weekdays (Mon–Fri): Daytime & evening availability (8:00 AM – 10:00 PM)
  • Saturdays: Full daytime & evening availability (8:00 AM – 11:00 PM)
  • Sundays: Afternoon & evening availability (after 1:00 PM)
  • Note: Not available Sunday mornings (prior to 1:00 PM) due to weekly parish services and fellowship.

Downtown Location & Parking

Conveniently situated at 105 Cottage Place (at the corner of Franklin Avenue and Cottage Place) in downtown Ridgewood:

  • Street & Municipal Parking: Street parking along Cottage Place and neighboring streets, plus nearby Village of Ridgewood municipal parking lots and the Hudson Street parking garage within walking distance.
  • Mass Transit Access: Less than a 5-minute walk from the Ridgewood NJ Transit train station and bus terminal.
  • Drop-Off & Loading: Convenient curbside drop-off and kitchen loading access on Cottage Place.

Policies & Insurance

We welcome a wide variety of civic, nonprofit, cultural, educational, and private gatherings:

  • Insurance & Diocese Guidelines: All rentals operate under Episcopal Diocese of Newark and parish insurance policies. Renters provide a standard Certificate of Liability Insurance (or special event policy).
  • Exterior Signage: Temporary directional signs for event guests are welcome during your booking; permanent or external organization signage is not permitted on the grounds.
  • Setup & Cleanup: Renters and caterers leave the hall and kitchen broom-clean at the conclusion of the rental block.

A Community Gathering Place Owned by Christ Episcopal Church

The Great Hall is owned and operated by Christ Episcopal Church, Ridgewood, and is open to any and all appropriate community groups, nonprofits, businesses, and private hosts regardless of religious affiliation. Rental proceeds directly support the maintenance of our historic property and the parish’s local community outreach ministries.
